Quick reference

The formulas you reach for most often.

Not a substitute for derivations — just the readouts worth having pinned while you solve numericals.

Electrostatics
F = kq₁q₂ / r²

Coulomb's law — force between two point charges separated by distance r.

Current Electricity
V = IR

Ohm's law — the backbone of every circuit numerical you'll solve.

Magnetism
F = qvB sinθ

Force on a moving charge in a magnetic field.

EM Induction
ε = −N(dΦ/dt)

Faraday's law — induced EMF from changing magnetic flux.

Ray Optics
1/v − 1/u = 1/f

Lens formula — sign convention is where most marks are lost.

Modern Physics
E = hf − φ₀

Einstein's photoelectric equation — energy of emitted electrons.
