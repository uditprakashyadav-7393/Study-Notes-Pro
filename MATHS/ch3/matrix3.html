Transpose of a Matrix

Topic 3 - Transpose

What is Transpose?

Transpose of a matrix is obtained by interchanging its rows and columns. That means rows become columns and columns become rows.

Example

Given Matrix A:

1 2 3
4 5 6
→

Transpose Aᵀ:

1 4
2 5
3 6

Rows of A become columns in Aᵀ.

Step-by-Step Explanation

  • First row (1 2 3) becomes first column
  • Second row (4 5 6) becomes second column
  • Resulting matrix has order 3 × 2

Important Properties

  • (Aᵀ)ᵀ = A
  • (A + B)ᵀ = Aᵀ + Bᵀ
  • (AB)ᵀ = BᵀAᵀ
  • (kA)ᵀ = kAᵀ
