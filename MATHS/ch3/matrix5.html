Elementary Row & Column Operations

Topic 5 - Matrices

What are Elementary Operations?

Elementary operations are simple changes we can perform on the rows or columns of a matrix. They are used to simplify matrices, solve systems of equations, and find inverse matrices.

Types of Operations

  • Interchange: Swap two rows or two columns
  • Scaling: Multiply a row or column by a non-zero number
  • Replacement: Replace a row/column by adding multiple of another

1. Row Interchange (R₁ ↔ R₂)

Swap two rows.

Row vs Column Operations

  • All three operations also apply to columns (C₁, C₂, etc.)
  • Row operations are mainly used in solving equations
  • Column operations are used in determinants (next chapter)

Why are they important?

  • Simplify matrices to easier form
  • Used in Gauss elimination method
  • Help to find inverse of matrix
  • Reduce matrix to row-echelon form
